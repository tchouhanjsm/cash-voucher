## First sign-in

1. Open the application link supplied by the product owner.
2. Sign in with your registered email address and the temporary PIN delivered to you separately.

## Daily use

- Create a voucher and verify that the application confirms it was saved to the server.
- Check the date, amount, cash-impact classification, vendor/category and any attached receipt before finishing.
- Use the Register and reports to review recorded entries; follow your agreed business process for approval and cash closing.
- Protect devices that contain business records. Sign out when using a shared device.

## Important offline warning

The application can hold unsynchronized entries in the current browser/device when the internet is unavailable. A pending entry is **not yet confirmed as saved to the central system**, and it is not part of the Google Sheet/Drive backup until it synchronizes.

Keep the same browser/device available, watch for the pending queue to clear after reconnecting, and use the application's queue export/recovery procedure if needed. Do not clear browser data, uninstall/reset the browser or switch devices while entries are pending. Contact the product owner if an item remains pending or rejected; do not silently re-enter it without checking for a duplicate.

## Backups, changes and support

The product owner manages hosting, backend deployment and backups. The product owner may release tested updates; the application link normally remains the same unless you are told otherwise. A visible backup status is not a substitute for reporting an issue or reconciling any missing voucher.

When contacting support, include the time, page/workflow, device/browser and non-sensitive error text. **Never send your PIN or another user's credentials.**
