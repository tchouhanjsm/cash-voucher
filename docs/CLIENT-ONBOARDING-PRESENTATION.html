If you stop the service

Contact support before the service end date to request your final data export. We will agree how to hand over CSV records and any included receipt files or backup materials.

Service access ends on the agreed date. We retain or delete operator-held copies according to the signed service terms and applicable requirements. Ask us to confirm the relevant export date and deletion/retention arrangements in writing before termination.

Common safe next steps

  • App link does not open: tell support your device, browser and the time you tried.
  • Login fails: stop after the displayed error and use the agreed reset process; do not send passwords.
  • A voucher looks missing or duplicated: do not re-enter it immediately. Check pending sync and contact support.
  • Backup or restore is uncertain: do not run setup again and do not restore over the source Sheet.
