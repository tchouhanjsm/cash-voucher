## Outcome and acceptance criteria

- Payment entry remains usable at a 320 CSS-pixel viewport without page-level horizontal overflow.
- Vendor and amount fields remain readable and do not collide with the remove-row action.
- Category and optional note fields stack at narrow widths.
- The remove-row action has a minimum 44 × 44 CSS-pixel target at narrow widths.
- Browser E2E verifies that `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is detected and motion durations are minimized.
- No backend/API, authorization, voucher schema, accounting semantics, persistence, or production behavior changes.

## Implementation

- `style.css`: narrow-width payment-row layout at 380px and below; improve field sizing and row-action touch target.
- `test/e2e.py`: check payment entry at 320px, field geometry and reduced-motion preference.
- `docs/UI-UX-REVIEW.md`, `docs/PRODUCT-ROADMAP.md`, `docs/HANDOFF.md`, `docs/PR-HANDOFFS.md`: record outcome, limits and follow-up.
- This extends existing responsive checks; it does not claim complete browser zoom or WCAG conformance.
