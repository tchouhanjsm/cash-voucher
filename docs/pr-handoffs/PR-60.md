## Key findings

1.  Highest risk is the gap between mocked/source-level confidence and live operational confidence.
2.  Offline entries are device-local until synchronized; backend backup does not cover them before
    sync.
3.  Backup success metadata does not prove a restorable snapshot; a witnessed restore drill is
    required.
4.  Server-side role enforcement is present, but a full action × role and expired-session matrix
    needs explicit evidence.
5.  Idempotency should be proven under commit-then-lost-response and cross-tab retry scenarios.
6.  XSS coverage is incremental; raw modal HTML is a trusted-caller contract and CSV formula
    injection requires separate tests.
7.  Audit CSV is limited to the latest 200 events; it is neither a full audit archive nor an
    accounting ledger.
8.  Live Google auth, Drive, restore, physical-device usability and assistive-technology behavior
    remain unverified.
