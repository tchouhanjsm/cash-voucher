# PR 65 Handoff — Recorded voucher movement report

## Outcome

Give managers and owners a source-traceable view of recorded voucher movement without claiming physical cash, profit, bank balance or tax liability.

## Acceptance criteria

- Backend enforces existing `viewAll` permission on the server.
- Valid inclusive date range and PAYMENT/RECEIPT and ACTIVE/CANCELLED/ALL filters are enforced.
- Report returns source voucher identity, date, counterparty, category, notes, type, status, amount, creator and cancellation reason.
- Selected-row totals are distinct from active-only totals; counts distinguish active and cancelled rows.
- UI has validation, loading, error/retry and empty states.
- CSV export neutralizes formula prefixes and leading tab/CR/LF and correctly quotes commas and double quotes.
- Tests cover denied staff access, invalid filters/dates, count/total consistency and CSV safety.
- No schema migration, source mutation, physical-cash inference, tax logic, deployment or production-data operation.
