## Security and failure review

- The current AuditLog is best-effort and is not a tamper-proof or complete before/after journal.
- The current Audit CSV is limited to the latest 200 events and is not a full audit archive or accounting ledger.
- Device-local outbox entries must not be silently included in server exports until synchronization is confirmed.
- Exports must not leak PINs, session tokens, secrets, unnecessary personal data or unrestricted Drive links.
- CSV must be defended against spreadsheet formula injection, not just quote-escaped.
- No tax treatment, GST/TDS classification, rate, threshold, statutory filing calculation or accounting mapping is inferred.

## Residual risks / not verified

- Receiving accountant's accounting product/version/import template is not yet known.
- Legal entity, registrations, accounting method and applicable tax requirements have not been confirmed; qualified Indian CA review is required before tax fields or calculations are implemented.
- Live Google permissions, backup restoreability, physical-device behavior and external accountant import are not verified.
- Current schema lacks explicit settlement account/method, invoice identifiers, structured tax fields, chart of accounts, double-entry journals and dated opening/closing balances.
- Daily cash close remains blocked pending owner approval of `docs/DAILY-CASH-CLOSE-DESIGN.md`.
